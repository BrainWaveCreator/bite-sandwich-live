Our Story

Every sandwich has a story.

BITE started with one question: what does a chimi sandwich taste like when it's made right? The answer starts with the pan de agua. A traditional Dominican water bread with a crispy crust and a soft, fluffy interior. There's no dining room by design. The counter is where the conversation happens.

The chimi is a Dominican street sandwich. Most people outside the DR have never heard of it. Ask about it at the counter and you'll get the whole story while your order is being made. That's the point.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently Asked.

What is a chimi? +

A chimi (short for chimichurri sandwich) is a Dominican Republic street food classic. Grilled meat, cabbage, tomato, and house sauce, served on soft pan de agua bread. Dominican, not Argentine. This "chimi" is the sandwich, not the herb sauce. At BITE, we serve the real thing: traditional Dominican chimi with our house salsa especial, made with chorizo, chicken, pork, or beef.

Where are you located? +

1576 SW 1st Street, in the heart of Little Havana, Miami, FL 33135. We're an open-air counter with no dining room and no reservations. Order at the counter or online for pickup.

What are your hours? +

Monday through Thursday: 10 AM to 10 PM. Friday and Saturday: 10 AM to 11 PM. Closed Sundays.

Do you deliver? +

Yes. We're on DoorDash and Uber Eats. You can also order directly from bitesandwich.com for pickup, which is the fastest and best-priced option.

Do you have vegetarian options? +

Yes. Our Grilled Cheese sandwich, French Fries, Tequeño Cheese Bites, Fried Cheese Balls, Natural Juices, and full coffee menu are all vegetarian.

What makes BITE's chimi different? +

Three things. First, our salsa especial de la casa, a house sauce we developed and don't share the recipe for. Second, real pan de agua baked to order, never pre-sliced. Third, green tomato instead of pickles or cucumber, the way it's done in the Dominican Republic. This is true Dominican chimi, made the way it should be.

Ready to order?

Available for pickup and delivery. Order through our site or find us on DoorDash and Uber Eats.
